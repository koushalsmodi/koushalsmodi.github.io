Predicting switch power consumption

Juniper Networks — Data Science Intern, May–August 2024

Investigated linear regression and other machine learning models — random forests, gradient boosting machines, and neural networks — for predicting switch power consumption. I compared them across mean squared error, root mean squared error, relative error, and distribution plots, explored research on techniques beyond traditional supervised methods, and built a Python script to read real-time power consumption from switches via API.
